Before You Book

What to Expect

Everything you need to know before your first appointment with B777 Auto Detailing.

☎ Still have questions? Call us
What should I expect on the day of service? +

We arrive fully self-contained at your location — no access to your home or utilities needed. Here's how it goes:

  • We text you 30 minutes before arrival
  • Setup takes about 10 minutes
  • You can go about your day — no need to supervise
  • We do a walk-around with you when finished before packing up
How long does a detail take? +

Times vary by service and vehicle condition:

  • Exterior Wash — 60 to 90 minutes
  • Full Detail — 3 to 5 hours
  • Ceramic Coating — 5 to 8 hours (full day)

Heavily soiled vehicles or larger trucks/SUVs may take a bit longer. We'll give you an accurate time estimate when we confirm your booking.

Do I need to prepare my car before you arrive? +

Just a few things help us give you the best result:

  • Remove personal items, car seats, and valuables from the interior
  • Make sure the car is accessible — not blocked by other vehicles
  • If you have a garage spot, leave it open (shade helps for ceramic coatings)
  • That's it — we handle the rest
Do you bring your own water and power? +

Yes — we are 100% self-sufficient. Our mobile unit carries a fresh water tank, a generator, a pressure washer, and all professional products. You don't need to provide anything or stay home.

What if it rains on my appointment day? +

Light rain won't stop an interior detail, but exterior work and ceramic coatings require dry conditions. If the weather is a problem, we'll reach out the evening before to reschedule at no charge — your deposit (if any) carries over automatically.

How often should I get my car detailed? +

It depends on how you use your vehicle, but here's a general guide:

  • Exterior wash — every 4 to 6 weeks
  • Full detail — every 3 to 6 months
  • Ceramic top-up — once a year after initial coating

Rideshare drivers and families with kids or pets typically benefit from monthly interior cleans.

Do you service trucks and large SUVs? +

Absolutely. We detail everything from compact cars to full-size trucks, lifted SUVs, and work vans. Pricing for larger vehicles starts slightly higher due to additional time and product — you'll see the adjusted quote during booking or we'll confirm it when we contact you.

What areas do you serve? +

We currently cover:

  • Edmond, OK (home base)
  • Oklahoma City (OKC) and surrounding neighborhoods
  • Yukon, OK

Not sure if we reach you? Call or text us at 572-205-6012 and we'll let you know.

Is there a cancellation fee? +

No cancellation fee — ever. Just give us at least 24 hours' notice so we can offer your slot to another client. Same-day cancellations are understood; we only ask that you try to let us know as early as possible.

Prefer to call or text?

We're available 7 days a week — usually respond within minutes.
